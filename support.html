Track My Plant Support

Questions, bugs or ideas? Email supportplanttracker@gmail.com.

How do I cancel Pro?

Pro is managed by Apple. On your iPhone, open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions, and choose Track My Plant. Cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date to avoid the next charge.

I bought Pro but don't see it.

Open the Pro screen in the app and tap "Already subscribed? Restore", signed in with the Apple Account you used to subscribe.

Why didn't my reminder arrive?

Check that notifications are on for Track My Plant in your iPhone's Settings, and that the plant has a care schedule set.

The AI health check failed.

Check your internet connection and tap "Retry". If it keeps failing, email us and mention what time it happened.

Where is my data?

Your plants, photos and chats are stored on your iPhone. There is no account. Deleting a plant removes its data; uninstalling the app removes everything. See the Privacy Policy.
